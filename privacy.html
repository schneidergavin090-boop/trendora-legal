Trendora – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Trendora is a private tool that runs only on the owner's own computer. It is used by the owner alone and has no other users.

What data is used

  • The access tokens issued by TikTok and Google after the owner logs in. They are stored only locally on the owner's computer and are never shared, sold or sent anywhere else.
  • The owner's own video files and their titles/descriptions, which are sent only to TikTok or YouTube when the owner starts an upload.

What is not done

  • No data from other TikTok or YouTube users is collected.
  • No analytics, tracking, advertising or cookies.
  • Nothing is published automatically.

Deletion

The owner can disconnect an account in Trendora at any time; the stored tokens are then deleted. Access can also be revoked in the TikTok or Google account settings.

Datenschutz (Deutsch)

Trendora läuft nur auf dem eigenen PC des Besitzers und hat keine anderen Nutzer. Gespeichert werden nur die Anmelde-Tokens von TikTok/Google – lokal, nie weitergegeben. Videos und Texte gehen nur beim vom Besitzer gestarteten Hochladen an TikTok bzw. YouTube. Keine Daten anderer Personen, kein Tracking, keine Cookies, keine Werbung. Trennen in Trendora löscht die Tokens.
