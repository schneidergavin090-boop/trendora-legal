Trendora – Terms of Service

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Trendora is a private, non-commercial software tool that runs locally on the owner's own computer. It is used by its owner only, to upload short videos he created himself to his own TikTok and YouTube accounts as drafts or private videos.

Use

  • Trendora is not offered to the public and has no other users.
  • Videos are only uploaded as drafts (TikTok inbox) or as private videos (YouTube). Publishing is always done manually by the account owner inside the official apps.
  • Only the official TikTok and Google/YouTube APIs are used, in line with their terms and developer policies.
